Why I built this

Entrance exam success isn't decided on one special day — it's built from the quiet accumulation of everyday effort. But keeping that up every day is the hardest part.

Even with good materials, deciding what to do today, when to review missed questions, and how much has piled up — working that out each time was a burden for children and parents alike.

Chuju Tore brings practice, review, and records into one place and leaves only the "flow that keeps going." No account needed, and your data stays on the device — so you can focus on the daily study with peace of mind.

— MRCY

FAQ

How much can I use for free?
The core features are free to use. If you'd like every level and your full history, you can subscribe to Plus.
How do I cancel Plus?
Plus is an auto-renewing subscription. You can cancel anytime from your App Store account settings. Turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. See the Terms of Use for details.
Do I need an account?
No. Download and start right away. Study data is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.
Is it safe for children to use?
Chuju Tore contains no ads or tracking and collects no personal information. Study data is stored on-device only. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Where is my data stored?
Study history, scores, progress, and similar data are all stored on your device. Nothing is sent externally. Uninstalling the app deletes the on-device data.
